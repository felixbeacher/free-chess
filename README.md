# Free Chess

Arrange your own opening, or choose a random formation, then play a casual computer opponent in your browser.

## Play

Visit https://felixbeacher.github.io/free-chess/ or download `index.html` and open it in a browser. The game is a single self-contained HTML file with no external libraries or accounts required.

You play White. Put your usual 16 pieces anywhere in ranks 1–4, or preview and reshuffle a random formation. The computer independently chooses its hidden Black formation before your setup. Reveal the board to begin; White moves first.

Normal movement, check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant and promotion are supported. Castling requires the king and rook to start on their usual squares. Hidden arrangements can begin in check or immediate checkmate. The in-game rules explain the details and draw conditions.

The computer is a casual opponent with a time-limited search running locally in the browser. This is single-player, not networked multiplayer. Reloading clears the game.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages serves `index.html` from the root of the `main` branch. No build step is needed.
